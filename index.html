FAQ

客户最常问的问题Questions clients ask most

我不会写文案，怎么办？I do not have the copy written. Can you help?
可以。你只需要用微信语音或文字把想说的告诉我，我来整理成页面文案。如果一句话都写不出来，我会先问 10 个问题，从你的回答里提取。Yes. Just send me a voice note or a rough message and I will shape it into page copy. If you are stuck starting, I will ask ten questions and build from your answers.
我没有照片和 Logo 呢？What if I have no photos or logo?
没有照片我会用字体与配色排版来撑住页面质感，或用可商用图库替代；没有 Logo 我可以顺手做一个简单的文字标。这些都是含在报价里的，不会临时加钱。Without photos I build the visual weight from typography and color, or use licensed stock imagery. Without a logo I will make a simple wordmark. Both are included — no surprise add-ons.
做好之后我自己能改吗？Can I edit it myself afterwards?
可以，源码完全归你。改动文字和图片只需要打开一个文件替换即可，我会附一份图文操作说明。如果你完全不想碰代码，也可以买年费维护。Yes, the code is fully yours. Changing text or images means editing one file, and I include a short illustrated guide. If you would rather not touch code at all, annual maintenance is available.
域名和服务器要多少钱？What do domain and hosting cost?
域名约 ¥60–90 / 年，静态托管可以用免费方案，两者加起来通常不超过 ¥100 / 年，由你自己账户支付，我不经手。A domain runs about ¥60–90/year and static hosting can be free — usually under ¥100/year total, paid from your own account so I never hold it.
付款方式，能开发票吗？How do I pay, and can I get an invoice?
50% 定金开工、50% 上线前结清，支持微信 / 支付宝 / 对公转账。个人可开电子收据，企业可开普票。50% deposit to start, 50% before launch. WeChat, Alipay or bank transfer. A digital receipt is available for individuals, and a standard invoice for companies.
不满意怎么办？What if I am not happy with it?
初稿不满意，可在提交前无理由退全额定金。进入两轮修改阶段后，如仍无法达成一致，按已完成工作量结算，剩余部分退还。If you dislike the first draft, cancel before revisions begin for a full deposit refund. Once revisions are underway, we settle for completed work and refund the rest.
